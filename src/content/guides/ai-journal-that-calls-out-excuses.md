**Yes. Aurelius Agent is an iPhone journal whose AI is written to be honest before kind.** He opens with substance, never praise. He keeps replies to a few sentences. And when the same excuse shows up on separate days, he names it and shows you the days it came from.

**Who it is for:** people who want an outside view that sees through their excuses, not a cheerleader.

## What does "honest before kind" sound like?

His instructions spell out the tone. Not "Great job staying consistent!" but something closer to "Seven days. The chain holds. Do not admire it. Extend it." Not a question about your focus score, but "You had the hours. You chose not to use them."

Short, plain sentences. No "great question". No emojis. Two to four sentences for a reply.

## Does it make things up about you?

It is told not to. He reflects only what you actually wrote. He will not say you slept badly or skipped the gym unless you said so. That matters: calling out an excuse only works if the evidence is yours.

## How does it catch a repeating excuse?

- **Each entry.** He answers your Morning Account and Evening Examination, often with a directive or one sharp question.
- **Your promises.** Things you list in What Must Be Done get asked about at night and marked Kept or Missed.
- **Over weeks.** When the same habit shows up on two separate days at least a week apart, he names it by what sets it off. Seen on five days, it is marked proven. Tap a day to read what you wrote.
- **On demand.** Tap him on any note and he asks one question about it. In chat, prompts like "What am I avoiding?" and "Hold me accountable." send on a tap.

## Is that too harsh for some people?

It can be. If you mostly want to vent and be heard, a gentler journal is a better fit; [Aurelius vs Rosebud](/guides/aurelius-vs-rosebud/) covers that choice. Aurelius is firm, not cruel. The Stoic model is Seneca forgiving the day's fault and telling himself not to repeat it.

He is not a therapist. In a crisis, a person or a local service comes first.
