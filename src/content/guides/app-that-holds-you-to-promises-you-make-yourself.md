**Yes. Aurelius Agent is an iPhone journal built to hold you to the promises you make yourself.** Each morning you name one to three things the day fails without. Each evening he asks about each one, and you mark it Kept or Missed. Over weeks he names the excuse that keeps coming back.

**Who it is for:** people who keep telling themselves they'll start tomorrow, and are tired of breaking their word to the one person who always notices.

## How does it work, day to day?

1. **Morning: name it.** In What Must Be Done you write one to three things, in your own words. Three is the limit on purpose. "+ For tomorrow" adds one to the next day, and a long-press moves today's line to tomorrow.
2. **Evening: answer for it.** With the section's reminder on, an evening message from Aurelius asks about each line. You mark it Kept or Missed.
3. **Write why.** The Evening Examination asks what you did well, what badly and what you left undone. He answers each entry.
4. **Weeks: see the pattern.** When the same reason shows up on separate days, he names it and shows you the days it came from.

## Why does a promise to yourself break so easily?

Because nobody asks. A promise to a friend has a witness. A promise to yourself has only you, and you are also the one making the excuse. Writing it down helps a little. Having it read back to you at night, in your own morning words, helps more. Seeing that the excuse is the same one you used three weeks ago helps most.

## Can it add a promise for me?

Yes. When you resolve to do something in chat, he can turn it into a **Hold me to it** button under his reply. One tap puts it on What Must Be Done, and it is checked like any other.

## What happens when I miss one?

You mark it Missed. That is all. No penalty, no money lost, no red screen. A missed day leaves a gap in the record, and your rank never falls. The point is an honest record, not a punishment.

## What else is in the app?

A Morning Account and an Evening Examination every day, an optional 30-second score of Energy, Focus, Body and Satisfaction, a Weekly Lesson with one challenge for the week ahead, and a chat that answers with your own journal in hand.

It is iPhone only. It costs $89.99 a year with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or $14.99 a month. It is not therapy.
