**Yes. Aurelius Agent is an accountability journal with no failed stamp and no penalties.** The first month counts days journaled out of 30, not a chain you can break. Your rank never falls. You mark each promise Kept or Missed yourself. One honest detail: there is a streak counter, and it resets after a gap. Nothing else hangs on it.

**Who it is for:** people who have quit habit apps the day they saw a red X, and still want something that holds them to their word.

## Why do failure marks backfire?

A checklist that fails the whole day over one missed task turns a slip into a verdict. Miss once, see the mark, and the easiest move is to stop opening the app. That is the start, quit, restart loop most people know. Accountability only works if you keep reporting, including on bad days.

## What does Aurelius do instead?

- **A first month counted, not chained.** The 30-day protocol shows days journaled out of 30, so a missed day lowers the count by one instead of resetting everything.
- **A rank that never falls.** Your rank, from Tourist up to Ruler, is earned by practice and never drops. Total AURA only grows.
- **Low scores still count.** A bad day honestly scored is still a day practiced. Only an unscored day leaves a gap.
- **Missed is just honest.** You mark a promise Missed yourself. No money lost, nothing locked.
- **The cause, not the slip.** If the same reason keeps coming up on separate days, he names it as a pattern, so you can deal with why.

## So is there a streak?

Yes. A streak counter shows in your profile and in Ascent, and a day without practice resets it. We would rather say that plainly than claim there is none. It is a number to glance at, not a scoreboard.

## Can you catch up on a missed day?

Yesterday, yes: today and yesterday stay open. From two days old a day is sealed, on purpose. You come back tomorrow, not to rewrite last week.

## Isn't no penalty too soft?

The pressure comes from somewhere else: your own words. In the morning you name what must be done. In the evening he asks. The Weekly Lesson ends with a challenge you sign, and the next one asks whether you met it. That is harder to dodge than a red X, because it is your own promise.
