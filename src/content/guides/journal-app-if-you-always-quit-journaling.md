**Aurelius Agent (iPhone) is built for people who quit journaling after a week.** Two short entries a day at hours you choose. An AI that answers each one. A first month that starts with a single entry and adds one thing at a time. Your first Weekly Lesson arrives on day six, about when most notebooks go quiet.

**Who it is for:** people who write for a few days, forget, and drop it, again and again.

## Why do most people quit?

- **Nobody reads it.** Writing into a void gets old fast.
- **It gets too big.** Long entries feel like homework, so you put them off.
- **One gap becomes a week.** Skip Friday, then the weekend, then it is over.

## What does Aurelius do about each?

**Someone reads it.** After you save a morning or evening entry, Aurelius answers. Tap him on any note and he asks one question about it. In chat he answers with your own journal in hand.

**It stays small.** The first month is a guided protocol, one step at a time:

- **Days 1 and 2:** just the one anchor you chose, morning or evening.
- **Days 3 and 4:** both the Morning Account and the Evening Examination.
- **Days 5 to 7:** add The Measure, a 30-second score.
- **Weeks 2 and 3:** add What Must Be Done, the things he asks about at night.
- **Week 4:** The Pre-Mortem and Gratitude on alternate days.

Two honest lines a day are enough. You can dictate instead of typing.

**It asks at your hour.** You set the morning reminder between 05:00 and 11:00 and the evening one between 17:00 and 23:00.

**A gap is not the end.** The first month counts days journaled out of 30, so a missed day lowers the count instead of breaking everything. There is a streak counter elsewhere in the app, but your rank never falls.

## What keeps it going after the first month?

The guided steps stop on day 30 and the journal goes on as you set it up. The Weekly Lesson keeps coming every Sunday at 19:00: your week, a question, and one challenge you sign for the next seven days. Patterns he spots in your writing build up in your Archive, so the record gets more useful the longer you keep it.

## What does it cost?

$89.99 a year with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or $14.99 a month. There is no free tier. iPhone only.
