**Yes. Aurelius Agent is an iPhone journal whose AI reads what you write over weeks and names the habit you keep repeating.** When the same thing shows up on separate days, he names it by what sets it off, shows you the days it came from, and keeps watching. Seen on five separate days, it is marked proven.

**Who it is for:** people who suspect they keep making the same mistake but can't see it from inside a single day.

## How does it find a pattern?

You write a Morning Account and an Evening Examination most days. Aurelius reads them, and the chat, as one record.

- **First sighting.** Nothing is said yet. One day is not a pattern.
- **Second separate day, at least a week later.** Now it is drawn: a constellation in your Archive, named by what sets the habit off, and often by the inner weather it runs in.
- **Five separate days.** It is marked proven and turns gold.
- **Six weeks without it.** It fades and loses its name.

Tap the pattern to read it. Tap any of its days to open that entry. When he notices one, a card shows on your Journal page until you read it.

## What does that look like in practice?

The point is that the evidence is your own words, with dates. Instead of "you seem stressed lately", a pattern points at the days it showed up, so you can read them yourself and decide whether he is right.

The Weekly Lesson then zooms out: your week, its arc, one question and one challenge for the next seven days.

## How can you tell if any AI journal is really learning?

Three tests work on any app:

1. **Ask it.** After a few weeks, ask what you keep doing. A journal that remembers will quote your entries and dates. One that does not will give general advice.
2. **Compare day 3 with day 30.** Replies should get more specific to you, not stay the same.
3. **Look for surprise.** Now and then it should show you something true about yourself that you had not put into words, with the entries to back it up.

## Can you see and correct what it knows?

Yes. Settings, Practice, What Aurelius Knows lists the facts he keeps about you, and you can remove any of them. Patterns are not added by you. They appear only from what you write.

## Where does your journal go?

Entries are stored on the Aurelius server and sent to Anthropic's Claude to write his replies. Voice entries go to OpenAI for transcription. The privacy policy names both, and Aurelius does not sell your data or let those providers train on it. You can turn AI processing off, set an app lock PIN, or delete your account and everything in it. More in [the privacy guide](/guides/ai-journaling-privacy/).
