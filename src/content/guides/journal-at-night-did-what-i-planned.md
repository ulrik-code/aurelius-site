**Yes. Aurelius Agent is an iPhone journal that checks your evening against your morning.** In the morning you write a Morning Account and list one to three things in What Must Be Done. At the evening hour you choose, he asks about each one and you mark it Kept or Missed. Then the Evening Examination asks what you did well, what badly and what you left undone.

**Who it is for:** people who plan well in the morning and lose the thread by lunch, and want something that asks at night.

## What do you write in the morning?

The **Morning Account**: what is on your mind, what waits, what could go wrong, before the day shapes you. Add **What Must Be Done** for the one to three things the day fails without. If you want, **The Pre-Mortem** asks how tomorrow breaks and the move that stops it; the next day it asks how it went.

## What happens at night?

- An evening message asks about each line in What Must Be Done. You mark it **Kept** or **Missed**.
- The **Evening Examination** asks what you did well, what badly and what you left undone.
- After you save, Aurelius answers. If he ends on a question, you can reply in chat.

Each entry opens with an inner weather slider, from storm to sun. The evening starts where the morning left it, so you can see how the day moved.

## When do the prompts come?

You set both. Morning between 05:00 and 11:00, evening between 17:00 and 23:00, in your timezone. Any section can have its own reminder.

## Can you skip the typing?

Tap the microphone to dictate an entry, then edit it before saving.

## Can you change it later?

Today and yesterday stay open. From two days old a day is sealed. You can't quietly rewrite what you said you would do.

## What does it cost?

$89.99 a year with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or $14.99 a month. iPhone only. It is a journal and a mentor, not therapy.
