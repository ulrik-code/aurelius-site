**Yes. Aurelius Agent (iPhone) has a practice called Impression for exactly this.** You write three things: what happened, what you told yourself about it, and what else it could mean. Then Aurelius separates the event from the story with you, in chat.

**Who it is for:** people who know the Stoic idea that events don't upset us, our judgments do, and want a way to actually do it in the moment.

## What is the Stoic impressions exercise?

Epictetus put it plainly: "Men are disturbed not by the things which happen, but by the opinions about the things" (Enchiridion 5). He tells his students to meet every harsh appearance by saying to it: "You are an appearance, and in no manner what you appear to be" (Enchiridion 1).

The practice has three steps:

1. **The bare event.** What a camera would have recorded. "My manager didn't reply to my message."
2. **The story.** What you added. "She thinks my work is bad. I'm going to lose this job."
3. **Other readings.** What else it could mean. "She is busy. She replies to everyone late on Fridays."

The relief comes from step 2. Once the story is written down next to the event, you can see how much of the weight was yours.

## How does Aurelius do it?

Tap the plus button and choose **Impression**. Write the three parts. Aurelius takes it into chat and works through it with you, pulling the event apart from the judgment you added.

## What about what is up to you and what is not?

That is the other half of Epictetus (Enchiridion 1), and the app has a practice for it too: **The Sort**. You empty your mind with Aurelius in chat, then mark each worry **Up to me** or **Not up to me** and seal it. Thirty days later what you set down comes back, and you mark **It happened** or **It never came**.

## Can you do this without an app?

Yes. Three lines on paper work. The app adds a second reader who questions the story, and a record you can look back on.

## What else is in the app?

A daily Morning Account and Evening Examination, promises you mark Kept or Missed each night, patterns he finds in what you write, and a Weekly Lesson with one challenge. It is iPhone only. It costs $89.99 a year with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or $14.99 a month. It is not therapy.
