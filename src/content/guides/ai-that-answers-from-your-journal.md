**Yes. Aurelius Agent is an iPhone journal with an AI chat that answers from your own entries.** He remembers your record across sessions. When he draws on a past entry, its date shows under his reply, and a tap opens that day. You can also tap him on any note for one question about what you wrote.

**Who it is for:** people who want to talk something through with someone who has actually read their journal, not a chatbot meeting them for the first time.

## What does the chat know?

What you have written: your Morning Accounts and Evening Examinations, notes, scores, the virtues you named, your aim for the day, and the patterns he has spotted. He keeps a list of facts about you, which you can see and edit under Settings, Practice, What Aurelius Knows.

He is told to reflect only what you actually wrote. He will not claim you did something you didn't say.

## How do you check what he is basing it on?

Dates under a reply open the journal entry he drew on. A pattern card in chat means he filed something in your Archive; tap it to read the pattern and the days it came from.

## What can you ask?

Anything about your own practice. Suggested prompts sit above the box and send on a tap, for example:

- "What am I avoiding?"
- "What pattern do you see in me?"
- "Where is my discipline weakest?"
- "Hold me accountable."
- "Prepare me for tomorrow."

When you commit to something, he can add a **Hold me to it** button, which puts it on your list for the day and asks about it that evening.

You can dictate instead of typing. He answers in the app language you choose.

## Are there limits?

Yes. There is a daily chat allowance, shown by a small gauge. When it runs out, chat resumes after the reset. Replies are short by design, a few sentences.

He is not a therapist or a medical service. In a crisis, a person or a local service comes first.

## Where does the conversation go?

Entries and chat are stored on the Aurelius server and sent to Anthropic's Claude to write his replies. Aurelius does not sell your data or allow its AI providers to train on it. You can turn AI processing off in Settings, Privacy.

## What does it cost?

$89.99 a year with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or $14.99 a month. iPhone only.
