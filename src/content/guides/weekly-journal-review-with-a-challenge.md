**Yes. Aurelius Agent writes you a Weekly Lesson from your own journal.** It gives your week a score with his verdict, tells the story of the week, asks one question, and ends with one challenge for the next seven days. You accept and sign it. The next Lesson asks whether you met it.

**Who it is for:** people who journal daily but never step back to see the week, and want one clear thing to work on next.

## What is in a Weekly Lesson?

You read it a page at a time:

- **The score and his verdict.** An AURA Level from 0 to 100, in plain words.
- **Your profile.** Discipline, Devotion, Reflection and Balance.
- **The story of your week,** with its inner weather.
- **Your chosen virtue,** and the long view once you have earned one.
- **The constellation:** patterns he has seen in your writing.

Then it asks:

- **Last week's challenge.** Met, or failed with one honest sentence.
- **One question.**
- **The Debt.** Name one person from the week and what they taught you.
- **Your new challenge.** Accept and sign, or decline.

## When does it arrive?

Your first Lesson lands at 19:00 on day six. During the first month it comes about every seven days. After that, every Sunday at 19:00 in your timezone. It arrives by notification and as a card on your Journal page, and every Lesson is kept in Archive, Lessons.

## What if the week was thin?

If too little was written, there is no reading. A short note says so. The Lesson quotes your own entries, so the more you write, the sharper it gets.

## How is this different from a weekly summary?

A summary tells you what happened. The Lesson ends in a commitment you sign, and the next one holds you to it. Challenges you meet count toward your rank, which only rises.

## What else does the app do?

Every day: a Morning Account, an Evening Examination, and What Must Be Done, with each item marked Kept or Missed at night. In between, a chat that answers from your journal.

It is iPhone only. It costs $89.99 a year with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, or $14.99 a month.
